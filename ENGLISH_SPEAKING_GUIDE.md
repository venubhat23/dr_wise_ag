## Essential Grammar Rules

### 1. Subject-Verb Agreement
**Rule:** Subject and verb must agree in number (singular/plural)

✅ **Correct Examples:**
- He **runs** fast (singular)
- They **run** fast (plural)
- The dog **barks** loudly
- The dogs **bark** loudly

❌ **Common Mistakes:**
- ~~He run fast~~
- ~~They runs fast~~

### 2. Present Tenses

#### Simple Present
**Use:** Habits, facts, routines
- I **work** every day
- She **likes** coffee
- Water **boils** at 100°C

#### Present Continuous
**Use:** Actions happening now
- I **am working** right now
- They **are studying** for exams
- She **is cooking** dinner

#### Present Perfect
**Use:** Past actions with present relevance
- I **have finished** my work
- She **has lived** here for 5 years
- We **have seen** that movie

### 3. Past Tenses

#### Simple Past
**Use:** Completed actions in the past
- I **worked** yesterday
- She **went** to school
- They **played** football

#### Past Continuous
**Use:** Ongoing actions in the past
- I **was working** when he called
- They **were sleeping** at 10 PM
- She **was reading** a book

### 4. Future Tenses

#### Will Future
**Use:** Decisions, predictions
- I **will help** you
- It **will rain** tomorrow
- They **will arrive** soon

#### Going to Future
**Use:** Plans, intentions
- I **am going to** study tonight
- She **is going to** buy a car
- We **are going to** travel

### 5. Articles (A, An, The)

#### A/An (Indefinite)
- Use **'a'** before consonant sounds: a book, a car, a university
- Use **'an'** before vowel sounds: an apple, an hour, an honest man

#### The (Definite)
- Use for specific things: **The** book on the table
- Use for unique things: **The** sun, **The** moon
- Use for superlatives: **The** best, **The** tallest

### 6. Prepositions

#### Time Prepositions
- **At:** at 5 o'clock, at night, at Christmas
- **On:** on Monday, on July 4th, on weekends
- **In:** in January, in 2024, in the morning

#### Place Prepositions
- **At:** at home, at work, at the station
- **On:** on the table, on the wall, on the street
- **In:** in the room, in London, in the car

### 7. Question Formation

#### Yes/No Questions
- **Do** you like coffee?
- **Does** she work here?
- **Are** they coming?
- **Have** you finished?

#### WH-Questions
- **What** is your name?
- **Where** do you live?
- **When** will you arrive?
- **Why** are you late?
- **How** do you do this?
- **Who** is that person?

### 8. Conditionals

#### Zero Conditional (Facts)
If + present simple, present simple
- If you heat water, it boils
- If it rains, the ground gets wet

#### First Conditional (Possible future)
If + present simple, will + verb
- If it rains, I will stay home
- If you study, you will pass

#### Second Conditional (Hypothetical)
If + past simple, would + verb
- If I won the lottery, I would travel
- If she knew, she would help

## Common Shortcuts & Informal Speech

### Everyday Shortcuts

#### Agreement/Acknowledgment
- **Formal → Informal**
- Yes → Yeah, Yep, Yup, Uh-huh
- No → Nah, Nope, Uh-uh
- Okay → OK, K, 'Kay, Alright

#### Greetings
- Hello → Hi, Hey, Yo
- How are you? → How's it going?, What's up?, Sup?
- Goodbye → Bye, See ya, Later, Catch you later

### Common Contractions

#### Be Verb Contractions
- I am → I'm
- You are → You're
- He is → He's
- She is → She's
- It is → It's
- We are → We're
- They are → They're

#### Have/Has Contractions
- I have → I've
- You have → You've
- He has → He's
- We have → We've
- They have → They've

#### Will Contractions
- I will → I'll
- You will → You'll
- She will → She'll
- We will → We'll

#### Negative Contractions
- Do not → Don't
- Does not → Doesn't
- Did not → Didn't
- Cannot → Can't
- Could not → Couldn't
- Would not → Wouldn't
- Should not → Shouldn't
- Will not → Won't
- Have not → Haven't
- Has not → Hasn't

### Informal Expressions

#### Instead of "I think"
- I guess...
- I suppose...
- I reckon...
- I figure...
- Seems like...

#### Instead of "I don't know"
- No idea
- Beats me
- Not sure
- No clue
- Dunno (very informal)

#### Instead of "I understand"
- Got it
- I see
- Makes sense
- Fair enough
- Roger that

### Texting/Chat Abbreviations
- LOL = Laughing out loud
- OMG = Oh my god
- BTW = By the way
- FYI = For your information
- ASAP = As soon as possible
- TBH = To be honest
- IDK = I don't know
- IMO = In my opinion
- TTYL = Talk to you later
- BRB = Be right back

## Vocabulary Building Strategies

### 1. Word Families
Learn related words together:

**Happy (adjective)**
- Happiness (noun)
- Happily (adverb)
- Unhappy (negative)

**Success (noun)**
- Successful (adjective)
- Successfully (adverb)
- Succeed (verb)

### 2. Synonyms (Same Meaning)

#### For "Good"
- Excellent
- Great
- Wonderful
- Fantastic
- Amazing
- Awesome (informal)

#### For "Bad"
- Terrible
- Awful
- Poor
- Horrible
- Dreadful

#### For "Big"
- Large
- Huge
- Enormous
- Massive
- Giant

#### For "Small"
- Tiny
- Little
- Mini
- Petite
- Microscopic

### 3. Common Phrasal Verbs

#### With "Get"
- Get up = wake up/rise
- Get on = board (bus/train)
- Get off = leave (bus/train)
- Get over = recover from
- Get along = have good relationship
- Get by = survive/manage

#### With "Look"
- Look after = take care of
- Look for = search
- Look forward to = anticipate
- Look up = search (dictionary/internet)
- Look into = investigate

#### With "Take"
- Take off = remove/depart (plane)
- Take on = accept responsibility
- Take up = start (hobby)
- Take over = assume control
- Take back = retract/return

### 4. Common Idioms

#### Business/Work
- **Think outside the box** = be creative
- **Hit the ground running** = start quickly
- **On the same page** = in agreement
- **Back to square one** = start over
- **The ball is in your court** = your decision

#### Daily Life
- **Piece of cake** = very easy
- **Break a leg** = good luck
- **Cost an arm and a leg** = very expensive
- **Once in a blue moon** = rarely
- **Spill the beans** = reveal a secret

### 5. False Friends (Confusing Words)

#### Accept vs Except
- **Accept** = agree to receive: I accept your offer
- **Except** = excluding: Everyone came except John

#### Affect vs Effect
- **Affect** (verb) = influence: The rain affects my mood
- **Effect** (noun) = result: The effect of rain is flooding

#### Their vs There vs They're
- **Their** = possession: Their car is red
- **There** = location: Put it there
- **They're** = they are: They're coming

## Quick Learning Tips

### Daily Practice
1. **Read** English news for 10 minutes daily
2. **Listen** to English podcasts/videos
3. **Speak** to yourself in English
4. **Write** a daily journal
5. **Learn** 5 new words per day

### Memory Techniques
1. **Use flashcards** for vocabulary
2. **Create sentences** with new words
3. **Group similar words** together
4. **Use mnemonics** for difficult words
5. **Review regularly** (spaced repetition)

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. ❌ Double negatives: ~~I don't have no money~~
   ✅ I don't have any money

2. ❌ Wrong word order: ~~I very like it~~
   ✅ I like it very much

3. ❌ Missing articles: ~~I am student~~
   ✅ I am a student

4. ❌ Wrong preposition: ~~Depend of~~
   ✅ Depend on

5. ❌ Literal translation from native language
   ✅ Learn English expressions as they are

## Pronunciation Tips

### Difficult Sounds
- **TH** sounds: Think (θ), This (ð)
- **R** vs **L**: Right/Light
- **V** vs **W**: Very/Wary
- **Silent letters**: Know, Write, Island

### Word Stress
- **Present** (noun) vs Pre**SENT** (verb)
- **Record** (noun) vs Re**CORD** (verb)
- **Import** (noun) vs Im**PORT** (verb)

### Intonation
- Rising tone for yes/no questions ⬆️
- Falling tone for statements ⬇️
- Rising-falling for WH questions ⬆️⬇️

## Practice Exercises

### Fill in the Blanks
1. I ___ (work) here since 2020.
2. She ___ (go) to the store yesterday.
3. They ___ (study) when I called.
4. We ___ (meet) tomorrow at 5 PM.

### Correct the Mistakes
1. He don't like coffee.
2. I am agree with you.
3. She is more taller than me.
4. They was happy yesterday.

### Answers
**Fill in:** 1. have worked, 2. went, 3. were studying, 4. will meet
**Corrections:** 1. doesn't, 2. I agree, 3. taller, 4. were
